            The previous post covered dsh-peer's connection direction and activity dashboard. This one goes after a narrower question: how do you interject while the other agent is in the middle of a turn, and even abort it?

            It took a while to see that DSH has three granularities of "sending a message": prompt, steer, and cancel. The difference is not in the network layer — it is in the agent loop's turn/step boundaries.
